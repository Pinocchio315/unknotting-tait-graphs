# Deposited records for manuscript versions 1.1 and 1.2

These files accompany **Computation of Unknotting Numbers: Which Knot Breaks the
Bernhard–Jablan Conjecture?**. They distinguish input ranges, completed lower-bound verdicts,
explicit upper-bound certificates, and exploratory data.

The current aggregation entry point is `../consolidate_results.py`. It reads
deposited inputs and writes only to `../generated/`; it does not replace the
historical aggregates in this directory.
The rebuilt counts are 1,227 exact values and 176 further improved ranges.
For v1.2, `generated/paper_v1_2/counts.tex` and six appendix-row files are
regenerated by the same script and included directly in the manuscript.
The read-only `../verify_manuscript.py` checks these inputs against exact
regeneration before comparing the completed manuscript with the data.

## Frozen comparison ranges

- `paper_v1_1_snapshot.json`: the historical research baseline assembled on
  September 8, 2026, for 12,965 knots. It uses package 2026.8.1 except for
  104 entries restored to their April 2026 ranges; 27 lower bounds are also
  restored from two to one. It is not an unmodified KnotInfo release.
- `paper_v1_1_manifest.json`: checksums and per-record provenance for the
  baseline and obstruction inputs.
- `reference_table_2026-09-07.json`: the earlier archived aggregate. The
  later baseline also restores the 27 original McCoy input ranges, so their
  contribution is visible in the historical comparison.
- `u_table_2026-09-07.json` and `consolidated_2026-09-07.json`: historical
  outputs, retained unchanged. Regenerate the current reports instead of
  using these older aggregates.
- `comparison/releases/`: compressed extracts of public `database_knotinfo`
  snapshots 2026.4.1, 2026.6.1, 2026.8.1, and 2026.9.1, with a manifest of
  public source URLs, wheel hashes, upload times, and extracted-file hashes.
- `comparison/gebel_prangley_2026.json` and
  `gebel_prangley_source_verification.json`: the published theorem lists and
  their primary-source attribution. All 194 exact values and 30 improved
  lower bounds agree; the 194 exact values are precisely the September
  exact agreements.
- `comparison/11a14_figure_verification.json`: embedded figure and reference
  PD codes, original-record and August package provenance, and the replayed
  `11a_14` figure diagnostic. Three SnapPy floating-point exterior comparisons
  identify the left, changed-left, and right diagrams up to mirror; exact
  Jones state sums on the 8- and 10-crossing references confirm their mirror
  polynomial equality. The reference unknotting values are dated tabulated
  inputs. Reproduce with `python audit_11a14_figure.py` from the repository
  root; the default run writes nothing and uses no live database.
- `comparison/crossing_formula_verification.json`: exact rational comparison
  of the stored determinant, signature, resistance, and self-linking data
  for 69,632 crossing changes; hashes identify the two compared inputs.
  Zero mismatches are a consistency check, not an independent recomputation
  of all diagram invariants or knot identifications.
- `comparison/baseline_comparison.json`: reproducible comparison counts,
  seven disputed intervals, and input hashes.
- `comparison/baseline_overrides.csv`, `september_comparison.csv`, and
  `mccoy_release_comparison.csv`: every baseline override, changed release
  comparison, and record in the 3,627-knot McCoy cohort, respectively.

Run `python audit_knotinfo_releases.py --verify` from the repository root
for a read-only reproduction. The 1,227 exact determinations split into
194 agreeing September exact values, six conflicting September exact
values, and 1,027 September unresolved entries. A seventh disagreement is
the non-exact interval for `13a_650`. These conflicts are reported rather
than silently combined with the computed lower bounds. Excluding the seven
conflicts, 97 retained upper bounds exceed the September values: 94 entire
intervals are weaker and three intervals are incomparable.

This provenance distinguishes historical-baseline improvements, agreement
with independently tabulated values, and unresolved release discrepancies.
Checksums detect input changes; they are not proofs of the mathematics or
an assertion that a live KnotInfo page has the same contents.

The snapshot retains `[1,3]` for `13n_3370`. The published bound `u <= 2` from
Brittenham–Hermiller, Theorem 1.3(a) and Lemma 2.5, is therefore supplied as a
separate input in `bernhard_jablan/brittenham_hermiller_upper_bounds.json`.
This file also records their upper-bound construction for `12n_491`.
The two new Greene lower bounds, combined with these existing upper bounds,
increase the exact-value count by two. In particular, the former construction
is not treated as a new upper-bound result of this paper.

| Other files in `open23/` | Flype checks, child-knot data, and supplementary exploratory classifications |

The Greene records give `u(12n_491) = u(13n_3370) = 2` and contribute two
exact values under method tag `G`. Together with `u(12n_288) = u(12n_501) = 2`
from `montesinos/`, they identify `13n_3370` as a counterexample to the
Bernhard–Jablan conjecture by
[Brittenham–Hermiller, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.05985v2).
Appendix H prints the exact correction terms for `12n_491` and the remaining
candidate sets for `13n_3370`, using one representative of each conjugate pair.
`verify_manuscript.py` compares all 52 printed representatives with these records.

The Appendix F conclusion is conditional on the Bernhard–Jablan property; its
806 knots are not part of the unconditional exact-value count. Historical
comparison and exploratory files, including those in `dkt/`, are preserved as
archival records and are not additional results of v1.1. Per-shard working files
are not deposited.

The verification commands in the directory READMEs exercise the mathematical
kernels, known examples, and deposited unknotting certificates. Rebuilding these
aggregate files does not rerun every obstruction or reidentify every crossing
neighbor used in the conditional Appendix F analysis.
